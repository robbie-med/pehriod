Pehriod logs your period the way a doctor would measure it, with one tap at a time, and keeps everything on your phone.

<b>One-tap bleeding log</b>
Pick pad, tampon, cup or liner once. After that, each change is a single tap on light, half or soaked. Absorbency is remembered and shown as colored droplets; tampons use the FDA sizes Light, Regular, Super, Super Plus and Ultra. Every change is scored with the Pictorial Blood loss Assessment Chart. A period of 100 points or more, or 80 mL in a cup, is heavy menstrual bleeding.

<b>Did it help?</b>
An hour after each painkiller, Pehriod asks how much it helped. Over a few periods you see which medicine works for you. Poor response to ibuprofen or naproxen is flagged for your doctor.

<b>Safe dosing</b>
24-hour limits are added up by ingredient across combination products, with minimum times between doses and warnings for products that shouldn't be combined.

<b>Ready for your appointment</b>
Answer a short plain-language questionnaire about your periods, pain, energy, pregnancy and birth control. The report prints in your language and your doctor's, with your logged data next to each answer.

<b>Optional trackers</b>
Weight, exercise, resting pulse, stress, travel, sickness, basal temperature, cervical mucus and ovulation tests, with 90-day trends.

<b>Private</b>
No account, no ads, no analytics. Nothing leaves your phone unless you export a backup.
